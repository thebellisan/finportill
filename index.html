Account & data deletion

Delete your FinPortill account

This page explains how to delete your FinPortill account and the personal data linked to it. FinPortill is developed by Bellisan.

Option 1: Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the FinPortill app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, send an email to office@thebellisan.com with the subject "FinPortill account deletion". Please write from the email address you use to sign in with Google or Apple. If you signed in with Apple's "Hide My Email", tell us that and include the Apple relay address shown in your account. We will confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email address and account identifier).
  • Your analysis history and saved reports.
  • Your watchlist and in-app preferences.
  • Any other personal data linked to your account in our active systems.

What may be kept

Subscription and billing records are held by Google Play, the Apple App Store and our subscription provider RevenueCat, and may be kept for as long as the law or the payment provider requires. We keep nothing else once your account is deleted, apart from encrypted backups, which are overwritten on a regular cycle (normally within 30 days).

Cancel your subscription separately

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Play Store > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > FinPortill) or in your Apple ID settings (Settings > your name > Subscriptions) so you are not charged again.

Delete some data without deleting the account

You can remove individual analyses and watchlist items inside the app. To request deletion of other specific data, email office@thebellisan.com.
